| 1 | R3 skipped original payouts: its condition required the member to be paid out already in the before-state, so only repayments were checked, although the comment said "every pot credit" | **Yes** | **Accepted, extended** | `test/circleRunner.js` R3 now covers every `PotCredited` (original payout) and every `EntitlementRepaid`. For each, the fee is computed independently from the gross amount. Collateral rise = held, withdrawable rise = gross − fee − held, held ≤ the amount after fee, and only members in good standing receive pot money. Original payouts also check that the member was unpaid before and is marked paid after, and that their entitlement equals the event's shortfall minus anything repaid in the same transaction. Repayments check that the entitlement falls by exactly the amount repaid. Per transaction: the computed fees equal the `FeeCharged` events and the treasury's rise. In `finalize`, where collateral returns and leftover shares also credit members, the collateral and withdrawable split is not compared; fees, treasury and entitlements still are. Extending the check surfaced one case the old condition had skipped silently: a member who catches up is back in good standing within the same transaction, and can be repaid its own old shortfall immediately. R3 now uses its status at the moment of the credit, and expects its collateral to rise by the catch-up refill plus anything held. The contract behaves correctly there; the earlier check just never looked. Coverage is proven, not assumed: a new test asserts that 5 of 5 original payouts, and the repayments of the short-payout scenario, pass through the check. A new shared mutation puts the same fee-rounding error into the contract **and** the simulator; parity cannot see it, and R3 catches it. |
| 2 | The evidence summary showed Slither as exit 0 because `echo "slither exit $?"` swallowed its status | **Yes** | **Accepted** | The step now captures Slither's exit code, prints it, and returns it, so the summary shows the real result (255 = findings reported). The runner keeps going with the remaining steps, as before. The findings are reviewed in `TEST_EVIDENCE_V9_2_2.md`; they are not called clean or vulnerabilities. |

**Unchanged:** contracts, collateral formula, factory and deployer architecture, simulator, app. No new deployment beyond v9.2.1's (a new factory is still required, because nothing has been deployed yet).
