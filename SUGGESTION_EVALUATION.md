# V9.1 suggestion evaluation log

> **Historical document.** Written for an earlier version, before the QIE mainnet deployment of 23 Nov 2026. Statements about what has or has not been deployed, which wallets to create and test counts describe that time. Current state: [`FINAL_RELEASE_READINESS.md`](FINAL_RELEASE_READINESS.md).

Each suggestion was checked against the v9 code before deciding. "Accepted" means implemented as proposed, "Modified" means implemented in a better form, "Rejected" means not implemented, with the reason.

| # | Suggestion | Decision | What was done, and why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First-seat worst case is wrong | **Accepted (real bug)** | v9's create screen measured seat 1's risk with a function that only models *earlier* seats defaulting; seat 1 has none, so it always returned 0 and the screen showed "collateral covers every round" at any cover, even 0%. Replaced by separate figures for (A) your own walk-away exposure and its cost to later members, (B) your payout shortfall if earlier seats default, (C) one earlier default, all earlier defaults, and an early leaver. `worstCaseForConfig` and `worstCase.js` were deleted so the wrong model can't be reused. |
| 1C | Multiple-default scenarios using actual contract rules | **Modified** | Instead of hand-written formulas, all scenarios run through `app/src/sim.js`, an exact mirror of `settle()`, `catchUp()` and `finalize()`. Every scenario states what it assumes. |
| 2 | Full economic simulation engine with a scenario matrix | **Accepted, with a stronger guarantee** | `sim.js` covers contributions, the collateral formula, cover, fees (including on later repayments), pre- and post-payout defaults, penalties, collateral draws, oldest-first repayment, catch-ups with late fees, netting, refunds and leftover distribution. `test/Sim.parity.test.js` runs 120 random scenarios (sizes 2–12, all covers, fees, defaults, catch-ups during rounds and in the cure window) through **both the real contract and the simulator** and requires identical balances to the base unit, plus exact money conservation. A deliberate one-number change to the simulator makes the test fail, so it is a real check. `scripts/payoff-matrix.mjs` generates `docs/PAYOFF_MATRIX.md` for sizes 2, 3, 6, 12 × every cover × every seat. Not modelled: timing details (when settle/catchUp are *allowed*), which only decide when calls happen, not what they do; "partial catch-up" doesn't exist (the contract takes the full amount or reverts, tested). |
| 3 | Reassess the last-seat collateral floor | **Rejected (kept), with reasons** | The last seat owes nothing after its payout, but it can still leave *before* its turn. A leaver forfeits up to one contribution from what it paid in plus its collateral. Without the floor, a last seat that stops in round 1 has paid nothing and locked nothing, so leaving is free while it shorts every earlier recipient until its own cancelled round refills the reserve. The floor is a joining bond: every seat locks at least one share × cover, so leaving early always costs something when cover > 0. The payoff matrix shows the effect (e.g. 6 members, 50%: last seat leaving early nets −0.5). The UI now calls it a joining bond. No formula change, so no redeployment is needed for this item. |
| 4 | Incentive compatibility, make strategic default less attractive | **Analysed and partly implemented** | Result (from the verified simulator): walking away after your turn gains exactly the uncovered part, about (N − 1 − s) × c × (1 − cover); at **100% cover the gain is 0 and nobody loses**, for one member or several coordinated wallets. No penalty that only uses money already in the contract can remove the gain below 100% without asking for more collateral. Implemented: (a) **`CircleFactory.minCoverageBps`**: a deployment can require full cover; production mode defaults to 100%, demo mode to 0% (**new factory deployment required**; existing circles unaffected); (b) every seat card shows its exact walk-away gain and what others lose; (c) 100% cover stays the default. Rejected: a penalty on leftover collateral of members behind (it only hits members who missed a few rounds, and rewards draining collateral first), reputation discounts on collateral (one person can have many wallets), and a reserve-funded protection layer (it would need outside money; the contract won't invent it). |
| 5 | Financial commitment preview: commitment / exposure / default scenarios | **Accepted** | New shared component `SeatRisk.jsx` with "Your commitment", "Your exposure after you receive the pot" and "What if someone stops paying". Used on the create screen (one card per seat), the seat tab, and referenced from the join screen. No green success messages for scenarios that assume others pay; 100% cover is shown in neutral text with its assumption. |
| 6 | Contract as source of truth | **Accepted** | Commitment and exposure come from `seatTerms()` when the circle has it, labelled "read from the circle contract"; older circles show "calculated from its settings with the same formula". Scenarios are labelled as simulations. All money maths uses bigint base units and the token's own decimals. Pending vs confirmed transactions were already shown separately (unchanged). |
| 7 | Product differentiation | **Modified** | Thesis tightened in the README: PotLoop is the savings circle where every member can see, before joining, exactly what they commit, what they could lose, and what happens if anyone stops paying, computed with the same rules the contract enforces. The new distinctive capability is the **What-if panel** (create screen and risk tab): pick who stops paying, when, and whether they catch up, and see every member's outcome, from a simulator proven to match the contract. None of the compared products (Money Fellows, Hakbah, MAF, Ajo Money, SUSU, ROSCASH) publishes member-level outcome simulation tied to enforceable settlement rules. We don't claim to have invented savings circles. |
| 8 | Demo around a realistic default | **Accepted** | `scripts/demo-mainnet.js` now tells the full story with real transactions at 20% cover: seat 1 takes the pot, stops paying, its collateral pays what it can, the next two recipients are paid short and the contract records what they are owed, seat 1 catches up in round 4 and the money goes straight to them, oldest debt first, and the circle finishes and records track records. |
| 9 | Deliver v9.1 with docs, changelog, handoff | **Accepted** | This folder and `potloop-v9.1.zip`. |

## Deployment compatibility

- **SavingsCircle**: unchanged from v9 (bytecode identical apart from compiler metadata). Existing v9 circles keep working with the v9.1 app.
- **CircleFactory**: one new constructor argument (`minCoverageBps`), so v9.1 needs a **new factory deployment**. Nothing has been deployed to mainnet yet, so nothing migrates.
- The app reads `seatTerms()` when present and falls back to the formula for older circles, clearly labelled.
